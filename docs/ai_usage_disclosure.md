AI assistance was used to plan the project, explain implementation steps, troubleshoot errors, and guide validation and testing.

## Limitations

These results confirm that the implemented checks passed; they do not guarantee that every business rule or edge case is covered. The reproducibility check was run against the starter dataset, not the full dataset.
